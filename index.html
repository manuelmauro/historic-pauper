Historic Pauper Deck Checker

Paste an MTG Arena deck export to check it against the Historic Pauper rules: every card must be legal in Historic and craftable with a common wildcard on Arena.

Rules
  • All commons on MTG Arena that are legal in Historic, including rebalanced and Alchemy cards.
  • A card is legal if it can be crafted with a common wildcard; you may play an uncommon or rare version of it. The paper Pauper ban list does not apply.
  • Main deck of at least 60 cards, sideboard of up to 15, at most 4 copies of any card except basic lands.

Banned

    All banned in Midweek Magic as of 5 May 2026.

      Not legal in Community Bo3 HP, though they were allowed in Midweek Magic.
